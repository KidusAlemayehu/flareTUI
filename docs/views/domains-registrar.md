# View 7: Domains & Cloudflare Registrar

Manage your domain portfolio registered directly through Cloudflare Registrar.

## Key Capabilities

- **Domain List**: View all active domains, expiration dates, auto-renew status, and transfer locks.
- **Domain Inspector (`Enter`/`i`)**: Detailed inspection of WHOIS contacts, DNSSEC status, registration dates, and assigned Cloudflare nameservers.
- **Transfer Lock (`t`)**: Toggle registrar lock status to protect domains against unauthorized transfers.
- **Auto-Renewal (`a`)**: Toggle automatic domain renewal on or off.
- **Register Domain (`n`)**: Interactive domain registration and onboarding modal.
