# Cloudflare Registrar Guide

Manage domains registered directly with Cloudflare at at-cost wholesale pricing.

## Capabilities

- **WHOIS Privacy**: Verify that Cloudflare WHOIS privacy protection is active.
- **DNSSEC Validation**: Confirm cryptographic signing of DNS zone records.
- **Transfer Lock**: Prevent unauthorized domain transfers (`t` to toggle).
- **Auto-Renew Protection**: Ensure critical business domains do not lapse (`a` to toggle).
